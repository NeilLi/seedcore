# Application Directions

Date: 2026-10-14
Status: Canonical portfolio decision — affordable physical AI, supervised STEM/maker adoption and OEM distribution

**SeedCore is an open trust runtime for affordable physical AI.** The next
commercial wedge is browser-first maker and supervised education kits with
bounded AI execution and verifiable action evidence. Delivery responsibilities
cover hardware qualification, OEM integration, trusted releases and field
support.

The three pillars are **SeedCore Inside** firmware/browser adoption,
**auditable classroom governance**, and a later **opt-in sim-to-real telemetry
flywheel**. The [strategy](robotics/physical_ai_strategy.md) owns the product
thesis; the [18-month plan](strategy/seedcore_physical_ai_execution_plan_2026_2028.md)
owns Oct 2026–Mar 2028 target windows. The [active queue](current_next_steps.md)
owns immediate sequencing.

## First Demonstrable Outcome

Show a proposed bounded move, its accountable principal and PDP result, admitted
token/session limits, attempted action and verifier disposition. Pair an allowed
case with out-of-envelope denial, active-session revocation, lost intent and
missing evidence. Denied input cannot reach motor dispatch; a modified plan
must be admitted under explicit transform semantics or re-evaluated.

The split-screen browser/arm hero demo is a target. Current `tree-lab.html`
contains no hardware, gateway or signed robot evidence connection. Physical
public demonstrations and customer trials require authority, evidence and
hardware acceptance for the exact profile. Keep fixtures visibly distinct.

## Capacity And Authority

Use one active physical profile and one live pilot initially. Keep the arm
fixture within a bounded bench scope or revise the capacity plan explicitly.
Advance kit production 10 → 25 → up to 100 cumulative units and school delivery
one → 3–5 sites only after QA, usefulness, cash and support gates.

SeedCore owns admission and proof. Microduck's onboard runtime retains balance,
bus and local safety; a valid token cannot override it. The separate MCU fixture
needs authenticated admission, local limits and an independent watchdog.
Motion, capture/egress, policy promotion and enrollment are distinct operations.

The [policy gates](policy_gate_matrix.md),
[token lifecycle](trust-runtime/execution_token_lifecycle_management.md) and
[flywheel boundary](seedcore_flywheel_harness.md) continue to apply. Proposed
wire fields, physical envelopes, conformance badges and adoption targets do
not become implemented capabilities through this portfolio update.
