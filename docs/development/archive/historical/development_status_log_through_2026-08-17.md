## Historical Interpretation

The stable conclusion across these periods is unchanged:

```text
AI intent is advisory.
The accountable Agent proposes an ActionIntent.
The PDP decides.
An ExecutionToken scopes the admitted attempt.
The actuator executes.
Evidence, replay, and the verifier close the loop.
```

Research, memory, model output, flywheel feedback, retrieval, simulations,
discovery, creative presentation, and digital-twin state do not become
authority by themselves.
