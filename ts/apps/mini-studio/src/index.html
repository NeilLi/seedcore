Guided lesson: make and rehearse a draft
  1. Choose Create synthetic draft fork in the local gallery.
  2. In Inspect, change the build name and save the draft revision.
  3. Open Simulation, wait for the model, and apply the rehearsal preset.
  4. Open Moments, create the tilt/return example, validate its curves, and rehearse it. Stop is available during the rehearsal.
  5. Inspect the captured result and use the slider to review samples.
  6. In DRC & BOM, recheck DRC and download the draft design ZIP. The assembly retains its gallery source and license.

This lesson uses a synthetic simulation. The separate evidence inspector requires a configured local service and a known governed attempt.
